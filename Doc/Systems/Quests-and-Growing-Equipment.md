# Quests and growing equipment

## Ordinary quests

`UQuestComponent` tracks one active quest and a separate completed-quest list. It does not write to `UTrialComponent` or Trial history. `UQuestDataAsset` defines a stable ID, required carried item and quantity, optional named enemy objective, base XP and item reward, and a free-skill-point bonus for completing the optional objective before turn-in. Inventory changes refresh the quest HUD through delegates; a credited enemy death advances the optional objective once for that enemy actor. The player returns to an `AQuestMarkerActor` to claim the reward. Starting and claiming use separate marker entries, so the player leaves and re-enters after meeting the required objective.

The current sample is `/Game/Data/Quests/DA_Quest_ForgekeepersRequest`. Its marker is in `/Game/NewMap` at `(-750, 500, 40)`, west of the player start. Collect the existing **2 Iron Ore** pickup, optionally defeat **TrainingEnemy** once, then return. The quest pays **75 XP** and **1 Trialbound Sword**; the optional defeat also pays **1 free skill point**. This is a collect-and-show quest: the ore stays in inventory after turn-in. The top-right quest HUD shows required and optional progress, reward preview, and a reason when the turn-in is blocked. A full inventory or excessive weight keeps the reward pending until room is available. Completed quests cannot pay twice. ## Trialbound Sword

`/Game/Data/Items/Data/Equipment/DA_Item_Weapon_TrialboundSword` is the named reward. It starts with **+22 physical weapon damage**. While it is equipped by its first owner, every **3 credited enemy defeats** increase its rank by one, to a maximum of **rank 3**. Each rank adds **+3 weapon damage**, so the weapon bonus progresses 22 → 25 → 28 → 31. The rank and defeat counter live on the `UWeaponItem` instance; the shared item data asset and ordinary Iron Sword do not change. Its inventory tooltip and Equipment combat breakdown show current growth. Melee Attack uses the current instance damage. The bound sword cannot be dropped or equipped by another owner.

The book supports exceptional named, soul-bound equipment growing with its owner (book 1, chapters 15–16). The sword name, defeat trigger, thresholds, and damage rates are **game design**, not quoted book rules. Quest rewards and item growth are session state; persistence remains deferred.
