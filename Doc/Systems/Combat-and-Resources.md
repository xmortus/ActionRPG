# Combat, resources, death, and effects

## Derived attributes and resources

`USecondaryAttributeComponent` owns maximum Health, Mana, Stamina, Armor, Crit Chance, Move Speed, typed damage resistances, and the **current** three resource pools. `/Game/Data/Attributes/DA_SecondaryAttributes_Base` supplies formulas: each value starts with a base and adds configured primary attribute scaling. Equipment and class secondary bonuses apply after the formulas. Primary/equipment changes trigger recalculation through delegates. On a maximum change, the current value is preserved and clamped; a stat change does not refill a resource.

Skills use `CanSpendResources`/`TrySpendResources` for mana and stamina. `RestoreResource` validates an actual missing amount; ordinary healing cannot revive a dead player. Passive recovery uses timers and configured rates/delays. Stamina recovery adds 5 per second for each earned Endurance to its base 20 per second, with the existing 1-second delay; Character Status shows the total and its breakdown. Death suppresses player health regeneration. Source: [secondary component](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/Components/Progression/SecondaryAttributeComponent.cpp), [formula asset type](../../Source/ActionRPG/Public/Progression/Core/SecondaryAttributeDataAsset.h).

## Attacks and damage

The player controller sends main-hand and offhand attacks through assigned skills. `USkillMeleeAttack` deals its skill base damage plus positive equipped main-hand `WeaponDamage` and Strength scaling; a damaging weapon supplies its damage type. Without that weapon, the skill's damage type applies. Ranged and magic skills create projectiles; the native `ASkillProjectile` handles direct hits and configured splash. Projectile creation failure cancels activation, including its resource cost, cooldown, and use XP.

Hits call Unreal `TakeDamage`. Player and enemy characters use the shared `FActionRPGDamageResolver` for valid positive damage and health reduction. Armor mitigates physical damage; typed resistance reduces matching incoming types. The training enemy uses timed melee attacks and can respawn after its configured delay. A successful damaging hit can generate training practice; enemy death can credit a combat trial. Sources: [melee](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/Skills/Implementations/SkillMeleeAttack.cpp), [projectile](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/Actors/SkillProjectile.cpp), [damage resolver](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/Combat/DamageResolution.cpp), [enemy](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/Characters/ActionRPGEnemyCharacter.cpp).

The Equipment combat section displays the **assigned main-hand skill's** effective attack damage and cooldown-derived attack speed (`1 / cooldown` attacks/s). Melee damage starts with skill base + main-hand weapon bonus + effective Strength × `MeleeDamagePerStrength`; its cooldown is base cooldown divided by a capped `1 + Agility × MeleeSpeedPercentPerAgility / 100` multiplier. The melee swing animation uses the same speed multiplier. Ranged projectile damage starts with skill base + effective Dexterity × `RangedDamagePerDexterity`; magic projectile damage starts with skill base + effective Intelligence × `MagicDamagePerIntelligence`. Each offensive skill then applies its own capped `1 + (skill level - 1) × DamagePercentPerSkillLevel / 100` mastery multiplier. The three current offensive skill assets use 3% per level, capped at 2×; these coefficients are game balance. Equipment listens for skill level-ups and uses the same damage methods that configure projectiles and melee hits. An unassigned skill shows `None`; a zero cooldown shows `No cooldown`. Source: [equipment stats](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/UI/Equipment/EquipmentStatsWidget.cpp), [melee implementation](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/Skills/Implementations/SkillMeleeAttack.cpp), [ranged implementation](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/Skills/Implementations/SkillRangedAttack.cpp), [magic implementation](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/Skills/Implementations/SkillMagic.cpp).

## Dodge and life state

The player dodge checks state, cooldown, and stamina, launches the character, and grants a short dodge window. Avoiding a live enemy hit or creating enough separation can count toward Agility practice, subject to training cooldowns. On lethal damage the player enters the dead state, stops movement, suppresses passive health recovery, and shows the death panel. Respawn is an explicit action after its configured delay. Death removes status effects marked `bRemoveOnDeath`. Source: [player character](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/Characters/ActionRPGPlayerCharacter.cpp), [death panel](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/UI/HUD/PlayerDeathWidget.cpp).

## Status effects

`UStatusEffectComponent` applies definitions such as `/Game/Data/Effects/DA_Effect_MinorRegeneration` and `DA_Effect_MajorRegeneration`. Each definition has duration, tick interval, per-tick resource deltas, optional primary bonuses, stack policy (`Refresh`, `Stack`, `Ignore`), maximum stacks, and death-removal behavior. Timers advance effects; attribute bonuses use reversible `Status.<EffectID>` sources. The two Regeneration effects add the source's effective Wisdom × `HealthPerTickPerWisdom` to each positive health tick before stacks; other effects opt into the coefficient separately, and negative ticks remain damage. The Effects tab displays the same effective healing amount. Resource changes update the HUD, and the status UI receives change and displayed-time events rather than polling every frame. Source: [effect component](../../Source/ActionRPG/Private/Components/Combat/StatusEffectComponent.cpp), [definition](../../Source/ActionRPG/Public/Combat/StatusEffectDataAsset.h).
